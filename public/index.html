How it works

  1. 1

    Reads the Acts

    454 sections of the Nigeria Tax Act, Tax Administration Act, Revenue Service Act and Joint Revenue Board Act, queried through Zetaris.

  2. 2

    Drafts

    A Nemotron researcher agent finds the sections that apply to your profile and must read each one in full before citing it.

  3. 3

    Checks

    A second Nemotron agent judges every claim against the quoted text and strikes what the text does not support.

Every model call carries a signed receipt you can inspect under Show working.
